# Workstream — EU Projects

## Objective
Individuare progetti UE finanziati o in avvio che possano generare incarichi retribuiti per Visual Design Studio: siti web, piattaforme, dissemination/communication support, visual/digital assets, maintenance o sviluppo collegato a WP/task/procurement.

## Discovery targets
- Horizon Europe and related EU programmes;
- PRIMA and Mediterranean programmes;
- Interreg and territorial cooperation;
- LIFE, CERV, Digital Europe and other relevant calls/programmes;
- projects with recent grant agreement/start date and unfinished digital presence;
- consortia where Communication/Dissemination WP ownership is identifiable.

## Required verification chain
Do not promote on programme/project name alone. Seek the strongest available chain:
`funded project → current/start timing → digital/web need → WP/task or procurement signal → beneficiary/owner → valid contact path`.

## Prioritized signals
1. explicit website/platform deliverable or task;
2. Communication & Dissemination WP with named leader/beneficiary;
3. project starts soon/recently and official site is absent/incomplete;
4. explicit procurement/tender/subcontracting path;
5. consortium partner with external communication/web capability needs.

## States
`DISCOVERED → VERIFYING → QUALIFIED → READY_FOR_REVIEW → READY_TO_CONTACT → CONTACTED → FOLLOW_UP → REPLIED → POSITIVE_REPLY_USER_ACTION_REQUIRED → MEETING → PROPOSAL → WON/LOST`

## Canonical storage
- project intelligence: `eu-projects/`;
- commercial opportunities: `opportunities/OPP-EU-*.json`;
- contacts: `contacts/`;
- research evidence/logs: `research/`;
- campaign metrics: `campaigns/`;
- operational views: `views/` and root `README.md`.

## Outreach policy
Prefer the beneficiary/person actually responsible for Communication/Dissemination, digital WP/task or procurement. Do not send generic consortium-wide email blasts. Check the global contact timeline before every first contact.

## EU daily pass
- scan newly funded / recently started projects;
- verify official project and beneficiary sources;
- identify WP/task ownership;
- inspect domain/site maturity and procurement/contact route;
- score VDS fit and freshness;
- promote only evidence-backed opportunities;
- prepare tailored outreach;
- update CRM and QA audit.
