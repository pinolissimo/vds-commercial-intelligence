# Workstream — Collaborations / Jobs

## Objective
Trovare incarichi e collaborazioni ad alto fit per Visual Design Studio: freelance/P.IVA/contract, outsourcing, white-label, partnership continuative con agenzie e software house, opportunità web/frontend/WordPress/IT e ruoli remoti compatibili.

## Geographic priority
- Italy: all 20 regions, with explicit territorial coverage tracking;
- Spain: all 17 autonomous communities plus Ceuta and Melilla;
- remote opportunities when commercially realistic and compatible with VDS capabilities.

## Discovery channels
- specialized job boards and freelance portals;
- LinkedIn and company career pages;
- agency/software-house collaboration pages;
- outsourcing/partner-network pages;
- current public listings indicating freelance, contractor, P.IVA/autónomo or external partner demand.

## Priority signals
1. active freelance/P.IVA/autónomo/contract opening;
2. explicit stable or recurring external collaboration;
3. WordPress/frontend/web development + infrastructure/IT overlap;
4. agency needing external delivery capacity or white-label support;
5. software house/communication agency with verified outsourcing model;
6. remote role with strong technical and commercial fit.

## Qualification
Verify separately:
- the listing/opportunity is current;
- the organization identity and official domain;
- the actual engagement model;
- VDS-specific fit;
- valid application/contact route;
- duplicate/suppression history.

Job-board text alone is not sufficient evidence for company identity or email guessing.

## States
`DISCOVERED → VERIFYING → QUALIFIED → READY_FOR_DAILY_OUTREACH_REVIEW → READY_TO_CONTACT → CONTACTED → FOLLOW_UP → REPLIED → POSITIVE_REPLY_USER_ACTION_REQUIRED → MEETING → PROPOSAL → WON/LOST`

## Canonical storage
- opportunities: `opportunities/OPP-IT-*.json`, `opportunities/OPP-ES-*.json` and other country prefixes when justified;
- companies/territories: `italy/`, `spain/` and future country folders only when needed;
- contacts: `contacts/`;
- research: `research/`;
- campaigns: `campaigns/`;
- operational views: `views/` and root `README.md`.

## Outreach policy
Every application/contact must be tailored to the verified opportunity and use the most relevant VDS proof: portfolio, EU-project experience, WordPress/custom frontend, performance-oriented VDS Engine capabilities, IT/infrastructure competence or software work as appropriate. Do not send generic bulk messages.

## Daily pass
- scan fresh postings and collaboration signals;
- verify organization and opportunity;
- score fit, freshness and revenue/continuity potential;
- deduplicate against the entire CRM;
- prepare/send only through permitted channels;
- track replies and follow-ups;
- refresh regional coverage and Next Best Actions;
- QA audit before reporting results.
