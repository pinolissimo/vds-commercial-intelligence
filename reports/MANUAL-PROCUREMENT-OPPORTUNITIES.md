# VDS — Manual Public Procurement / Tender Opportunities

> Separate manual track for public administrations, government entities, municipalities, universities/research bodies and other regulated buyers. These opportunities are **never part of automated/direct commercial email outreach**.

**Updated:** 2026-08-30  
**Owner:** USER / MANUAL PROCUREMENT REVIEW

## Why this track is separate

A public tender can have excellent VDS fit and economic value but still be unusable unless the bidder satisfies the procurement documents. Before any decision, verify PCAP/PPT or equivalent documents, eligibility, professional/economic/technical solvency, references, staff requirements, guarantees, registrations, deadlines, award criteria, required signatures/certificates and electronic submission procedure.
