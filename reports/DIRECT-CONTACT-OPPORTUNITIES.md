## Hard operating rule

Every first contact remains blocked until the final pre-action gate is rerun: **freshness → organization identity → correct recipient/route → global dedup → primary + emergency suppression → Gmail/Hostinger Sent history → opportunity-specific personalization**.

A published application form/platform takes precedence over a generic email. No guessed addresses.
