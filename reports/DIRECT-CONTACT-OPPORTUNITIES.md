## Contact operating model

For each organization the final Partner Hunt pass must perform: **freshness → correct recipient/route → global organization-level dedup → Sent + suppression check → opportunity-specific personalization → contact/application**.

Direct contact does not mean generic bulk email. Where an official application form/platform is explicitly required, that route takes precedence over unsolicited email.
